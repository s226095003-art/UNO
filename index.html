UNO Rules & Cards

Objective

Be the first player to get rid of all the cards in your hand!

On Your Turn

Match the top card on the Discard Pile by Color or Number/Symbol. If you don't have a matching card, draw one card from the Draw Pile.

Special Action Cards

Skip: Skips the next player's turn.
Reverse: Reverses the direction of play.
Draw Two (+2): Next player draws 2 cards and misses turn.
Wild: Allows you to change the active color.
Wild Draw Four (+4): Change active color + next player draws 4 cards and misses turn.

Calling "UNO!"

When you have only 1 card remaining, click the CALL "UNO!" button before playing your second-to-last card!
